Common questions

Everything is designed for clarity before enrollment.

Are these courses beginner friendly?

Yes. Each course starts with foundations, then moves into guided weekly execution, portfolio tasks, and practical outcomes.

How do I confirm payment?

After submitting the checkout form, you will be redirected to WhatsApp with your course, amount, selected method, and learner details already encoded in the message.

Can I enroll from anywhere in Pakistan?

Yes. The academy is online-first and supports local Pakistani payment methods for convenient enrollment.

Where is my enrollment data stored?

This standalone deployment stores demo leads in the browser using LocalStorage. The admin panel reads the same local data and can export leads to CSV.
